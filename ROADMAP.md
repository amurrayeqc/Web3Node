# Roadmap

CIDNest is maintained as a focused content-addressed storage service.

## Now

- Strengthen corruption, restart, and peer-failure integration tests
- Document storage quotas, replication behavior, and operator recovery
- Publish a constrained demonstration that cannot be used as unrestricted public storage

## Next

- Pluggable persistence backends
- Replication health and repair reporting
- Signed export/import manifests

## Not planned

- Anonymous unmetered public uploads
- Claims of permanent availability without an operator-owned storage policy
